What Changed?

The Dice Game sets in motion a chain of events that changes the future of the Pandavas, Kauravas and the Kuru kingdom.

👑
01

The Pandavas Lose Their Kingdom

Yudhishthira loses his wealth and kingdom during the dice games, changing the position of the Pandavas within the Kuru kingdom.

↓

Thirteen Years Away

After the second dice game, the Pandavas are required to spend twelve years in exile followed by a thirteenth year in concealment.

The Family Conflict Deepens

The events of the dice games deepen the conflict between the Pandavas and Kauravas and make reconciliation increasingly difficult.

The Path Toward Peace Becomes Difficult

The consequences of the dice games become part of the larger conflict that later leads to attempts to resolve the dispute.
